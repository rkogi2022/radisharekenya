Radishare Kenya Website Privacy Policy

At Radishare Kenya, we believe that youth-led and community-driven organizations, alongside innovative MSMEs, have the power to transform society. Our mission is to empower grassroots CBOs, youth networks, and MSMEs through mentorship, digital learning, consultancy, and strategic support.

We do this by providing:

  • Capacity building for CBOs in health, climate action, governance, and resource mobilization.
  • MSME development programs with a focus on e-commerce, agriculture, climate-smart enterprises, and digitization.
  • Consultancy services in research, communications, systems development, mentorship, and digital transformation.

Central to this mission is our community-first approach, ensuring that the people, businesses, and organizations we serve benefit from safe, transparent, and respectful data use. To serve effectively, and during the course of our interactions, we may collect information that helps us improve user experiences, customize services, and strengthen program delivery.

This Privacy Policy is our commitment to transparency, accountability, and protection of user information. It governs how Radishare Kenya and its affiliates collect, use, maintain, and disclose data from users of our website and digital platforms.

Definitions
  • Affiliate – Refers to partner CBOs, MSMEs, and collaborating organizations working with Radishare Kenya.
  • Radishare Kenya – Refers to Radishare Kenya and/or its affiliates.
  • Site – Any website, e-learning platform, consultancy portal, or digital resource owned and/or operated by Radishare Kenya where this policy applies.
  • SSL – Secure Sockets Layer, the standard security technology for encrypting user data.
  • User – Any individual, CBO member, MSME, or client who accesses the Radishare Kenya Site and its content.
Personal Identification Information

We may collect personal information from Users when they:

  • Visit the Site or interact with our e-learning platforms
  • Register for mentorship, consultancy, or MSME programs
  • Apply for funding or partnership opportunities
  • Subscribe to newsletters or program updates
  • Fill out surveys, forms, or feedback tools
  • Access digital learning or advisory resources

Information collected may include name, email, phone number, organization details, business sector, and role. Sharing this information is voluntary but may be required to access certain services. We may also collect non-sensitive, automatic data such as IP address, browser type, device details, and access times to help us improve services.

Non-Personal Identification Information

We may collect non-personal information (e.g., browser type, ISP, technical specifications) to analyze service usage and improve accessibility.

Web Browser Cookies

Our Site may use cookies to improve user experience. Users can choose to refuse cookies or receive alerts when cookies are sent.

How We Use Collected Information

Radishare Kenya may use collected information for purposes such as:

  • For CBOs: Tailoring mentorship, governance, and community health initiatives.
  • For MSMEs: Designing business support tools, training, and digitization opportunities.
  • For Consultancy Clients: Delivering tailored research, strategic planning, and communication services.
  • To facilitate access to e-learning and mentorship platforms.
  • To personalize user experience and improve our digital tools.
  • To send relevant updates (funding opportunities, training invitations, research findings).
  • To respond to inquiries and provide technical support.
  • To strengthen partnerships and improve service offerings.
How We Protect Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard all personal and business data against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

Third-Party Websites

Our Site may link to partner institutions, funders, or service providers. Radishare Kenya is not responsible for third-party practices. Users are advised to read third-party policies before sharing information.

Children’s Privacy

Radishare Kenya complies with local and international child protection laws and does not knowingly collect information from children without consent.

Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect new programs, legal requirements, or digital innovations. Updates will be posted on the Site’s homepage.

Acceptance of Terms

By using the Site, Users signify their acceptance of this Privacy Policy. Continued use after updates will be considered acceptance of the changes.
