CBO Mentorship Program by Radishare Kenya

Radishare Kenya, with support from donors, fundraising, and internal revenue, launched a CBO Mentorship Program to strengthen Community-Based Organizations (CBOs) across Kenya.

What We Do

Building on our mentorship model from Kaloleni Young Mothers (KYM) CBO, we provide structured guidance to CBO founders and leaders. To demonstrate impact, we mentor Ten CBOs at a time for free over a three-month period before scaling through additional funding.

Who Can Join

CBO founders and leaders seeking tailored mentorship in governance, financial management, resource mobilization, digital transformation, and community engagement.

Program Timeline

January 2025: Pilot mentorship with KYM CBO.
First Cohort (Jan–Mar 2025): 5 CBOs onboarded.
April 2025 & Beyond: Expanding to 10 CBOs per quarter.

How We Deliver Mentorship

The program is fully digital, using platforms like WhatsApp, web-based content, and Zoom for interactive learning. A Special Expert Session will also be scheduled to improve the quality of the mentorship program with key experts in various fields.

Program Expectations

  • All CBOs are expected to participate fully and commit to the program for a duration of 3 months.
  • CBOs are encouraged to identify a Champion for each module from her members.
  • CBOs are encouraged to all participate in the common class involving all other CBOs.
  • CBO members are encouraged to utilize the web course provided on Radishare website to learn and unlearn.
  • All CBOs are encouraged to pin a featured post on CBO mentorship by Radishare.
  • All CBOs are encouraged to feature a page about Radishare on their website.

Why This Matters

CBOs in Kenya face challenges such as:

  • ✅ Financial Constraints: Limited funding & poor financial management.
  • ✅ Weak Structures: Lack of governance & strategic planning.
  • ✅ Capacity Gaps: Limited fundraising, project management & digital skills.
  • ✅ Community Engagement Issues: Difficulty mobilizing support & maintaining transparency.
  • ✅ Legal & Compliance Barriers: Complex registration & tax obligations.

Key Focus Areas

We equip CBOs with skills in:

  • ✅ Organizational Development – Leadership, governance & legal compliance.
  • ✅ Financial Management – Budgeting, fundraising & sustainability strategies.
  • ✅ Impact Measurement – M&E frameworks & data-driven decision-making.
  • ✅ Resource Mobilization – Proposal writing & strategic partnerships.
  • ✅ Digital Transformation – Social media, online fundraising & tech integration.
  • ✅ Community Engagement – Advocacy, grassroots mobilization & inclusivity.
  • ✅ Operational Efficiency – Risk management & process optimization.

This program empowers CBOs to scale impact, improve sustainability, and drive meaningful change in communities across Kenya.
